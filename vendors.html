Directory FAQ

Common questions

How are vendors vetted?
Every vendor verifies their business identity, public reviews, and recent event history before going live. We re-verify annually and remove listings that fall below our response-time and customer-satisfaction thresholds.
How does booking work?
Browse, save vendors to a shortlist, and request quotes. Vendors respond directly inside Eventbook, where you can compare offers, sign contracts, and confirm. Anything you book is added to your event plan automatically.
Who handles payment?
You can pay vendors directly, or use Eventbook's optional payment service for deposits, milestone payments, and pay-after-event arrangements. Payment terms are always set by the vendor.
What happens with cancellations?
Each vendor sets their own cancellation policy, shown clearly on every profile. Eventbook records the policy at time of booking, so you and the vendor share a single source of truth if anything changes.
