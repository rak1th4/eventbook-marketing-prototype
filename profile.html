About Sundial Studio

Sundial is a two-photographer team based on Austin's east side, working in a quiet documentary style with a soft, light-led palette. We've covered around 240 weddings across Texas since 2019, with the majority in Austin, Hill Country, and Marfa, and a steady stream of milestone birthdays and corporate parties through the year.

Every package includes a same-week sneak peek (10–15 photos) and a final gallery of 600–900 edited images delivered within 6 weeks. Engagement sessions, second-photographer add-ons, and printed albums are optional add-ons.

Vendor FAQ

How far in advance should we book?
Saturdays in October–November and April–May usually book 6–9 months out. Other dates 3–4 months is comfortable.
Do you travel outside Austin?
Yes — across Hill Country, Marfa, and Houston without a travel surcharge under 90 minutes from Austin. Beyond that, we add a flat travel fee.
What's included in the gallery?
600–900 edited images depending on package, delivered via private gallery for download, sharing, and printing.
