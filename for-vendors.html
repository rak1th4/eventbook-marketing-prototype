Vendor FAQ

The questions vendors ask first.

How do I get paid?
Whichever way you and the planner agree. You can keep your existing payment flow (invoice, transfer, card) or use Eventbook's payment service for deposits, milestone payments, and pay-after-event arrangements. Funds go directly to you minus standard processing fees if you use ours.
What happens when a planner cancels?
Your cancellation policy is what governs the refund — it's recorded at booking time and shown clearly to the planner. Eventbook administers it on your behalf and resolves disputes with both sides looking at the same agreement.
Can I list in multiple cities?
Yes. Add as many cities as you actually serve, with travel terms set per city. We don't charge extra per city during early access.
Am I required to use Eventbook for payment?
No. The payment service is optional. Many vendors use Eventbook for discovery and quotes, and keep their existing payment processor. The planner sees the same booking either way.
Which event types can I serve?
Whichever you'd like — weddings, birthdays, corporate, parties, religious or cultural celebrations, fundraisers. Tag your listing with the event types you want to receive inquiries for.
When does free pricing end?
We'll give vendors 90 days' notice before introducing pricing in any city. No retroactive fees, no surprise commissions on bookings made under early access.
What does 'automated final payment' actually mean?
When a booking is confirmed, you set the deposit amount and the date the final payment is due (typically before or right after the event). Eventbook charges the planner's saved payment method automatically on that date, so you don't have to send a final invoice or wait for them to remember. Both sides get notified.
What if a planner cancels?
Your cancellation policy is signed at booking and stored alongside the contract. If a planner cancels, the policy governs what they owe and what gets refunded. Eventbook administers the refund or retention automatically. You set the terms once, they apply to every booking.
