Frequently asked

Questions, answered.

Does Eventbook work for events that aren't weddings?
Yes. Eventbook started broad on purpose. The same workspace, vendor directory, and AI assistant work for birthdays, corporate offsites, milestone parties, fundraisers, and the rest. Templates adjust to the event type so you start with the right tasks and budget shape.
How does the vendor directory work?
Browse vetted vendors across 11 categories and 35 cities. Filter by event type, price range, and availability, save shortlists, request quotes, and add chosen vendors directly to your event plan. Booking and payments stay tracked alongside everything else.
Do I have to pay vendors through Eventbook?
No. You can keep paying vendors however you and they prefer — invoice, transfer, card. Eventbook tracks the totals either way. If you'd like, we also handle deposits and milestone payments for you.
Can I invite collaborators?
On every plan. Free includes up to 3 collaborators per event. Pro removes the limit and adds permission roles (viewer, contributor, owner) and comment threads on every line item.
What does eveAI actually do?
Drafts vendor shortlists from your brief, balances your budget against your priorities, writes outreach emails, summarises long vendor threads, and answers planning questions. It works with your live data — your vendors, your dates, your budget — not a generic chatbot.
How does pricing work for teams?
Pro is $24 per planner per month. Add seats anytime — billed pro-rata. Enterprise covers agencies and venue groups with consolidated billing, SSO, and a dedicated CSM. Vendor listings remain free during early access.
Can I export my data?
Always. Export budgets, tasks, and guest lists as CSV. Export the full event plan as a printable PDF. Your data is yours, and that doesn't change if you cancel.
