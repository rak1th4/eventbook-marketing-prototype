About wedding photography in Austin

What to expect when booking in Austin

Average wedding photography in Austin runs $2,800 to $5,200 for an 8-hour day, with most established studios sitting in the $3,200–$4,000 range. Hill Country and ranch venues add a 10–20% travel and overtime premium during peak weekends.

Peak season runs October through early November and again April through May. Most photographers in this list book 6 to 9 months in advance for Saturdays and 3–4 months ahead for Friday or Sunday dates. Rehearsal dinner and engagement-shoot add-ons are common; same-day sneak peeks and second-shooter packages are typical at the higher end.

Popular venues nearby: Vista West Ranch, Mercury Hall, Prospect House, Kindred Oaks.

Photographer FAQ — Austin

How much does a wedding photographer cost in Austin?
Most wedding packages in Austin run $2,800 to $5,200 for 8 hours of coverage. Established studios with two shooters and a same-week sneak peek usually start around $3,400. Hill Country venues add a travel surcharge during peak weekends.
How early should I book?
Saturdays in peak season (Oct–early Nov, Apr–May) book 6 to 9 months in advance. Friday and Sunday dates are typically available 3 to 4 months out, with last-minute openings appearing more often in summer.
Do photographers in Austin offer engagement sessions?
Most do. Engagement sessions are usually offered as an add-on ($400–$900) or bundled into the higher-tier wedding packages. Some include the digital files only; others provide a small printed album as part of the package.
What's the typical turnaround for the final gallery?
4 to 8 weeks is standard, with same-week sneak peeks available from most studios. Higher-volume teams sometimes deliver in 3 weeks; album-led photographers may take up to 10.
Are travel fees normal for Hill Country venues?
Yes. Vendors typically include travel within 30 miles of Austin and charge a flat surcharge ($150–$350) beyond that. Overnight stays are standard for venues more than 90 minutes out.
